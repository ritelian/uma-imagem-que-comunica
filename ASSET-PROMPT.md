## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one finished original educational campaign poster, portrait ratio A3, full bleed flat graphic design (not a photo of a poster). Portuguese school year 9 visual communication example. Cream paper background, cobalt blue and acid lime with black. Central bold paper-cut metaphor: a tall smartphone silhouette becomes a birdcage with simple bars; one small lime bird escapes through an open door towards upper right. Sophisticated but reproducible with paper collage, minimal flat shapes, subtle paper texture, generous negative space. Exact large typography in lower third: "DESLIGA." and below it "VOLTA A VOAR." Small bottom text: "O teu tempo é teu." No other text, brands or watermarks. Powerful hierarchy, striking contrast, editorial Swiss-inspired composition.
